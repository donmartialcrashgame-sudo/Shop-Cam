Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Shop Camzon (“Shop Camzon”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you when you use the Shop Camzon website and services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Shop Camzon, we may collect information such as your name, email address, account information, shopping and order information, delivery information, and communications you send to our support team.

When you sign in with Google, Google may provide us with basic account information that you authorize, such as your name, email address, and profile information. We use this information to create and maintain your Shop Camzon account and provide the requested sign-in service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, authenticate, and manage your Shop Camzon account.
  • To process and manage purchases, orders, deliveries, and customer support requests.
  • To communicate with you about your account, orders, services, and important changes.
  • To provide, maintain, secure, and improve our website and services.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and other security problems.
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Google Sign-In and Google User Data

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Shop Camzon uses Google’s authentication service to verify your identity. We only request and use the Google account information needed to provide account authentication and related Shop Camzon services.

We do not sell your Google user data. We do not use Google user data for advertising or unrelated purposes. Google user data is handled only as described in this Privacy Policy and in accordance with Google's applicable policies and Limited Use requirements.

4. How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate Shop Camzon, such as authentication, hosting, database, payment, delivery, communication, security, and technical service providers. These providers receive only information reasonably necessary for the services they provide.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the rights and safety of Shop Camzon or others, or to investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so we encourage you to use a strong and unique password for services where passwords are used and to protect access to your email account.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

Shop Camzon may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, improve functionality, and provide essential website features. You can control certain browser storage and cookie settings through your browser.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or request restrictions on the use of certain personal information. You may also request information about how your data is processed. To make a privacy request, contact us using the support contact provided by Shop Camzon.

9. Children's Privacy

Shop Camzon is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use online shopping services in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Third-Party Services and Links

Shop Camzon may use or link to third-party services. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms. This Privacy Policy applies to Shop Camzon and does not control the privacy practices of independent third parties.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our services or legal requirements change. When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

12. Contact Shop Camzon

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Shop Camzon handles personal information, please contact Shop Camzon through the support/contact options provided on our website.
