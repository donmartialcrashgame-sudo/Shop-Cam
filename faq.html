Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common Shop Camzon questions.

How do I place an order?

Add a product to your cart, open Checkout, enter your delivery details, select a payment method and confirm.

Can I use different currencies?

Yes. Shop Camzon supports USD, Nigerian Naira and CFA/XAF display.

How do I view my receipt?

Open My Orders and select View Receipt on the order you want.

How do I report an order problem?

Use Report an Issue from the Support section and include the relevant order.

How do I contact customer care?

Open Customer Care to start a support conversation.
